Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. What we collect

When a visitor sends a message to a chatbot built by Vizara Studio, we collect the message and the bot's reply. If the visitor chooses to give an email address so a human can follow up, we collect that address as well. We do not ask for an email address to answer a question — it is only collected when the visitor volunteers one for a human follow-up.

We do not collect payment card details. Payments are handled by a hosted payment provider, and no card number ever reaches the chatbot or our console.

2. How conversations are used

Conversations are stored so that the chatbot's answers can be improved over time and so the business running the bot can review what customers are asking and step in where needed. That review is the main reason a conversation is kept at all.

3. Who processes the data

We use a small number of named service providers to run the chatbot:

  • Groq — generates the chatbot's answers.
  • Render — hosts the chatbot service.
  • Neon — stores the conversation database.
  • Formspree or Resend — sends the notification email when a conversation is escalated to a human.

These providers process data on our instructions and only for running the service. They may store data in countries other than Pakistan.

4. We do not sell data

We do not sell conversations, email addresses, or any other personal data, and we do not share them for advertising. Data is used to run and improve the chatbot for the business that owns it, and for nothing else.

5. Deletion requests

A visitor can ask for their conversation and email address to be deleted by emailing vizarastudio3@gmail.com. Tell us which site the conversation was on and roughly when it happened, and we will remove it.

6. How long we keep it

Conversations are kept while the chatbot subscription is active. When the subscription ends, the client's content and conversation data are deleted from our systems within about one week, except where we are required to keep records by law.
