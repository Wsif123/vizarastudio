Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. What the service is

Vizara Studio provides a support chatbot for websites. The chatbot is installed on your site, answers visitor questions from content you supply, and hands the conversation to a human when it is not confident. The service is sold as a monthly subscription together with a one-time setup fee for building and installing the bot.

2. Who can use it

You may use the service if you own the website it is installed on, or are authorised to act for the business that owns it, and you are able to enter into a contract. You are responsible for the content you give us and for making sure it is accurate and lawful to publish.

3. Answers are generated, and can be wrong

Answers are generated automatically from your own content. The system is built to stay close to what you have supplied and to escalate when it does not know, but generated answers can still be incomplete, out of date, or occasionally wrong.

The chatbot supplements your team's judgement; it does not replace it. Keep a human channel open for customers, review conversations, and correct your content when something is wrong. The bot does not give legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice.

4. Setup fees

The one-time setup fee is due before work starts. It covers the labour of collecting content, configuring the bot, testing, and installing it on your site. See the Refund Policy for when it can and cannot be refunded.

5. Plans, conversation allowance, and overage

Each plan includes a monthly conversation allowance, as stated on our pricing page at the time you subscribe. Conversations are counted monthly. Usage above the allowance is billed as overage on the following invoice.

We may change plan prices or allowances with at least 30 days' notice. A price change does not apply to a period you have already paid for.

6. Cancellation

Either side may cancel the subscription with 30 days' written notice. The service continues until the end of the notice period. Amounts already billed are handled under the Refund Policy.

7. Your content, and what happens when you leave

The content you provide remains your property. You grant us permission to store, process, and use it to run and improve the chatbot for you. We do not resell it or use it to build a bot for another business.

When the subscription ends, your content and chatbot data are deleted from our systems within about one week, except where we are required to keep records by law.
